# How to use SENDA, step by step

SENDA plans a crewed outing on Mars in four steps, shown on the bar at the top of the map:
**Goals → Rover → Spacewalks → Start**. A green check marks each finished step.

## 0. Open the mission
On the Mars globe, pick **Mount Sharp · Gale** in the site list and press **Explore base**.
**View Mars** (top right) goes back to the globe.

## 1. Goals: choose where to go
- Tap a rock (science target) on the map to see its card, then add it. Tap again to remove it.
- The **My goals** card lists them. Drag them to set the priority order (first = most important).
- **Base Alpha** card: **Change** moves the base; the map tool "Move base" does the same by tapping the map.
- **Quick Demo** (header) loads 3 targets at once; **Reset** clears goals, routes and scenario.

## 2. Rover: drive to the science sites
- **Your vehicle** card: set the **Driving speed** (slower uses less battery).
- Press **Find rover routes**. SENDA proposes parking spots near the goals and three routes:
  **Safest**, **Science** and **Quickest**. Pick one in the comparison card; the map draws it.
- After changing goals, base or danger zones, press **Recalculate routes**.

## 3. Spacewalks: walk from the rover to the rocks
- In **Rover stops**, choose a parking spot and press **Plan spacewalk**.
- Compare the three walking routes and pick one. **Next stop** plans the next parking spot.
- Every parking spot needs a planned spacewalk before you can start.

## 4. Start: final check and simulation
- The launch card shows the weather verdict, rover battery and suit oxygen left at the end.
  If there is not enough to come back, pick easier routes or fewer goals.
- Press **Start mission** to run the simulation.

## Simulation
- **Pause / Resume** and the speed buttons (1x, 4x, 15x) control time.
- **Something changed?** simulates a problem: path blocked, dust storm, or suit leak (only on foot).
  SENDA finds a new route from where you are.
- **Return to rover** and **Return to base** end the walk or the trip early.
- At the end, the **Mission summary** shows goals done, battery and oxygen used. Completed science
  procedures are saved in **Procedure history** inside My goals.

## Map tools (left of the map)
- **Select**: tap a rock to see it.
- **Move base**: tap the map to place the base.
- **Mark danger zone**: tap to add a circle the routes must avoid; tap it again to remove it.
  **Clear danger zones** removes all of them.
- **Scout waypoint**: tap anywhere to preview a safe rover path from the base.
- **2D / 3D** switch at the bottom of the map; **+ / −** zoom.

## Other header buttons
- **Crew**: astronaut names, team name and suit health.
- **NASA Labs**: extra tools (suit Monte Carlo, ground radar, sample rack, quantum optimizer).
- **Gale EZ & SEV**: background on the exploration zone and the crew vehicle.
